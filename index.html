Apps I've made, free for anyone to use.

  • Rattlesnake

    A weightlifting log for your phone. Each exercise starts with the sets, reps and weight from your last time on that machine, so you only change what's different. Your workouts are saved on your phone, not online.

    Open Rattlesnake Source code

  • Intonation

    Interactive labs on the math and sound of tuning: why some chords ring and others wobble, where commas come from, and how tuning systems differ.

    Open Intonation Source code

  • SAT Regression Trainer

    Practice for the SAT math questions you can solve with a Desmos regression: 95 question types, each with a worked example. It expands Sceptrell's Desmos Regression Trainer.

    Open the trainer Source code

  • Kiosk

    Puts a simple phone form in front of a Google Sheet, so many people can update it at once without breaking it. It has search and a barcode scanner, and was built for marching band uniform fittings. Kiosk runs on your own server, so there's no shared copy to open here.
